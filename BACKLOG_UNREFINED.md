## Preference Profile

- **BL-01 — Pool / facility access** · *Status: unrefined*
  Which days/times swimming is actually possible (pool opening hours), gym access, indoor-trainer vs. outdoor availability. Lets the coach avoid prescribing a swim on a no-pool day or an outdoor ride when only a trainer is available.
  *Area:* Preference Profile (SP0 schema + SP2 onboarding/edit).

- **BL-02 — Max single-session length & two-a-days** · *Status: unrefined*
  A cap on any one session's duration; whether the athlete can do two-a-days; max sessions/day. Bounds how the coach packs weekly volume into the available time windows.
  *Area:* Preference Profile.

- **BL-03 — Per-sport frequency / volume split** · *Status: unrefined*
  Minimum frequency or weekly hours per discipline (e.g. swim ≥ 2×/week, or an explicit hours split across swim/bike/run). Shapes balance across the three sports.
  *Area:* Preference Profile.

## Goals (Goal Manifest)

- **BL-04 — Race priority (A / B / C) & multiple target races** · *Status: unrefined*
  Support a season with a primary **A-race** plus secondary **B/C** races (priority levels), instead of a single target race — so the coach can periodise toward multiple events. For now the goal targets **one primary race**.
  *Area:* Goal Manifest (SP0 schema + SP2 goal form).
